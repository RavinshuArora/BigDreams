UNDERSTANDING SUBCLASS 485

Make an informed decision about your post-study options.

The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) is designed for eligible international students who have completed qualifying studies in Australia. Depending on the stream, it may allow eligible graduates to live, work and study in Australia temporarily.

The right pathway depends on the qualification you completed, your age, visa history and other requirements. Reviewing these details early can help you understand what needs to be prepared for your application.

VISA STREAMS

Which subclass 485 stream may be relevant to you?

  • Post-Higher Education Work stream: For eligible international graduates with a qualifying degree-level or higher qualification from an Australian institution.
  • Post-Vocational Education Work stream: For eligible graduates with an associate degree, diploma or trade qualification relevant to an occupation on the applicable skilled occupation list.
  • Second Post-Higher Education Work stream: For certain eligible holders of a previous graduate visa who studied and lived in a designated regional area.

Stream availability and requirements depend on your individual circumstances. You generally cannot change streams after applying.

IMPORTANT TO CHECK

Key requirements to review

  • Age: The general age limit is 35 or under when applying, although exceptions apply to some applicants.
  • Visa history: You generally need to have held a Student visa in the six months before applying, along with meeting the applicable visa criteria.
  • Eligible Australian study: Your qualification and study must meet the applicable course and study requirements.
  • English: You may need to provide evidence of the required English language ability, unless an exemption applies.
  • Other documents: Health insurance, an Australian Federal Police check and other supporting documents may be required.
  • Application timing: You generally need to have completed your eligible qualification within the six months immediately before applying. Check the rules for your stream.

Requirements and exceptions vary by stream and applicant. Check the current Department of Home Affairs rules before applying.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Subclass 485 visa FAQs

General answers to common questions about the Temporary Graduate visa.

What is the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)?

The Temporary Graduate visa allows eligible international students to live, work and study temporarily in Australia after completing qualifying studies. The applicable requirements depend on the stream.

What are the main subclass 485 streams?

The main streams are the Post-Higher Education Work stream, the Post-Vocational Education Work stream and the Second Post-Higher Education Work stream for eligible graduates with qualifying regional study and visa history.

How long can I stay on a subclass 485 visa?

The stay period depends on the stream, qualification and circumstances. The Post-Vocational Education Work stream generally allows a stay of up to 18 months. Post-Higher Education Work stream periods vary by qualification, and the Second Post-Higher Education Work stream may provide an additional 1 to 2 years for eligible applicants. Special arrangements may apply to eligible Indian nationals under the Australia-India Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement. Always check the current rules for your circumstances.

What is the age requirement for subclass 485?

Applicants generally must be 35 years of age or under when applying. Exceptions apply to certain applicants, including some Masters research and doctoral graduates and holders of Hong Kong or British National Overseas passports. Check the current criteria for your stream.

What English test score is required for subclass 485?

For IELTS tests taken on or after 7 August 2025, the general requirement is an overall score of 6.5, with at least 5.5 in each component. Other approved tests have different minimum scores. Test validity, exemptions and the requirements applying to your application should be checked against the current Department of Home Affairs rules.

When should I apply after completing my course?

You generally need to have completed your eligible qualification within the six months immediately before applying. Your Student visa history, course completion evidence and other eligibility requirements must also be considered. Do not leave your application until the last moment.

Can I work in Australia on a subclass 485 visa?

Eligible visa holders can work in Australia temporarily, subject to the visa conditions. You generally need to find your own employment.

Can I include family members?

Eligible members of your family unit may be included in an application or may be able to apply later as subsequent entrants, depending on the circumstances and applicable requirements.

Does a subclass 485 visa guarantee permanent residency?

No. The subclass 485 is a temporary visa and does not guarantee permanent residency. Any future visa pathway has separate eligibility requirements.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. Visa decisions are made by the Department of Home Affairs. Eligibility and outcomes depend on the applicant's individual circumstances and the rules in force.
