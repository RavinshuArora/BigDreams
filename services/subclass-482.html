Employer sponsorship

Work in Australia under the sponsorship of an approved employer, using your skills

Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand) is an employer-sponsored temporary work visa that allows an approved Australian employer to sponsor a skilled worker from overseas to fill a workforce need. The relevant visa stream, nominated occupation, qualifications and work experience all play a part in determining whether this pathway may be suitable. Depending on the circumstances and applicable requirements, it may also provide a pathway to explore permanent residence.

  • Requires sponsorship by an approved employer and nomination for an eligible position.
  • Allows eligible skilled workers to work temporarily in Australia for their sponsoring employer.
  • Get guidance on documents, eligibility and application preparation.
  • Eligible family members may be included, subject to the applicable visa requirements.

Discuss your eligibility

Eligibility criteria

A Subclass 482 application generally requires the following:

  • Employer sponsorship and nomination: You must be sponsored and nominated by an approved Australian employer for a position that meets the applicable requirements.
  • Occupation and visa stream: The occupation and criteria depend on the relevant Skills in Demand stream.
  • Qualifications and experience: You must meet the skills, qualification and work experience requirements that apply to your nominated occupation and stream.
  • English proficiency: You must meet the applicable English requirements or qualify for an exemption.
  • Health and character: You must meet Australia's health and character requirements.

Visa criteria, occupation settings, income thresholds and exemptions can change. Requirements should be checked against current Department of Home Affairs information for your circumstances.

Key considerations

Key features of the Subclass 482

  • Work in Australia: Eligible visa holders can work in the nominated role for their sponsoring employer, subject to visa conditions.
  • Possible permanent residence pathway: Some visa holders may later be eligible to explore employer-nominated permanent residence, such as Subclass 186, if they meet its separate criteria.
  • Family: Eligible family members may be included, subject to the relevant requirements.
  • Australian work experience: The visa may provide an opportunity to gain work experience in Australia.

These features depend on individual circumstances and visa conditions. No visa outcome or transition to permanent residence is guaranteed.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Helpful starting points for common employer sponsorship questions.

What is the Subclass 482 Skills in Demand visa?

It is a temporary employer-sponsored visa that allows an eligible Australian employer to sponsor a skilled worker for a nominated position, subject to the requirements of the relevant stream.

Do I need an employer to sponsor me?

Yes. The Subclass 482 pathway generally requires an eligible employer to sponsor and nominate the worker for a position that meets the applicable requirements.

Are there different streams under the Subclass 482 visa?

The Skills in Demand visa has Core Skills, Specialist Skills and Labour Agreement streams. The requirements differ between streams, so the appropriate criteria need to be checked for each case.

Is every occupation eligible?

No. Occupation requirements depend on the relevant stream and the rules in force at the time. The current official criteria should be checked for your circumstances.

Can the Subclass 482 visa lead to permanent residency?

Some visa holders may be able to explore a permanent residence pathway, such as the Subclass 186 visa, if they meet its separate requirements. It is not automatic or guaranteed.

Can I include my family?

Eligible family members may be included in an application, subject to the relevant visa requirements and conditions.

How long does the process take?

Processing times vary depending on the application type, completeness, employer and individual circumstances. Current official processing information should be checked before applying.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. No migration agent can guarantee a visa decision. Advice and assistance should be based on your individual circumstances and the current rules.
