Employer sponsorship

Work in Australia under the sponsorship of an approved employer, using your skills

Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand) is an employer-sponsored temporary work visa that allows an approved Australian employer to sponsor a skilled worker from overseas to fill a workforce shortage. When your occupation, qualifications and experience meet Australian standards, it can be an important starting point both for working in Australia and, where the conditions are met, for transitioning to permanent residence.

  • Requires the sponsorship of an approved employer and nomination for a specific occupation.
  • Employer-sponsored temporary work that can lead to permanent residence where the conditions are met.
  • Get guidance on documents, eligibility and application preparation.
  • Your family members can also live, work and study in Australia.

Discuss your eligibility

Eligibility criteria

A Subclass 482 application generally requires the following:

  • Employer sponsorship and nomination: You must be sponsored and nominated by an approved Australian employer for a specific occupation.
  • Occupation list: Your occupation must be on the relevant skilled occupation list.
  • Qualifications and experience: You must hold the qualifications and work experience required for your occupation.
  • English proficiency: You must meet the English requirements or qualify for an exemption.
  • Health and character: You must meet Australia's health and character standards.

The visa name, occupation lists and experience and English thresholds change over time; they are verified against current Department of Home Affairs sources for your circumstances.

Key considerations

Key benefits of the Subclass 482

  • Work in Australia: You work temporarily in your occupation with your approved employer.
  • Pathway to permanent residence: Where the conditions are met, you can move to pathways such as employer-nominated permanent residence (Subclass 186).
  • Family: Your family members can also live, work and study in Australia.
  • Australian experience: The experience you gain in Australia strengthens your career both locally and internationally.

These benefits are general in nature, and no visa outcome or transition to permanent residence is guaranteed.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Helpful starting points for common employer sponsorship questions.

What is an employer-sponsored visa?

It is a visa pathway where an eligible Australian employer sponsors or nominates a worker, subject to the requirements of the relevant visa stream.

Do I need an employer to sponsor me?

Employer sponsorship pathways generally require an eligible sponsoring employer. The exact requirements depend on the visa stream.

Is every occupation eligible?

No. Occupation requirements depend on the visa stream and rules in force at the time. Check the current official criteria for your circumstances.

Can this pathway lead to permanent residency?

Some people may have a pathway to permanent residency, but it is not automatic and depends on meeting the separate requirements.

How long does the process take?

Processing times vary depending on the visa type, application completeness and individual circumstances. Current official processing information should be checked before applying.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. No migration agent can guarantee a visa decision. Advice should be based on your individual circumstances and the current rules.
